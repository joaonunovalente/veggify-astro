## Why you'll love this recipe

Smoky barbecue, sweet pineapple, and a crisp-edged crust — this is the pizza I make when I want the house to smell like a summer cookout. Smoked jackfruit shreds like pulled pork and drinks up barbecue sauce in minutes, so you get all of that slow-cooked flavor without ever turning on the smoker.

The pineapple is not a garnish. Its sweetness cuts straight through the paprika and chipotle, and it caramelizes in the oven until the edges turn jammy and a little sticky. Fresh basil and a handful of arugula go on after baking, which keeps the pie bright and makes it taste like a salad, not just a slice of bread with toppings.

Best of all, this is a 45-minute dinner that beats the delivery version on every count, and it holds up better than a meat-topped pie does for leftovers.

## How to make this recipe

Get the oven hot first. A pizza stone, or an upside-down heavy sheet pan, needs a good 20 minutes at 450 °F so it can radiate heat straight into the dough. That is the difference between a crisp bottom and a pale one.

While it heats, sear the shredded jackfruit in a hot skillet with the smoked paprika, chipotle powder, and a tablespoon of oil. Give it four to five minutes, stirring every minute or so, until the strands are lightly charred at the edges. The char is flavor, not a mistake.

Then stir in the BBQ sauce and let it bubble for a minute. Taste it here rather than after baking — once it is on a salty crust with salty sauce, it is too late to fix, and you will almost always find you need less salt than you expect.

Stretch the dough, brush it with oil, and spread a thin layer of sauce, leaving a proper border so the crust can puff. Load on the jackfruit, the diced pineapple, and the cheese, then bake for 10 to 12 minutes.

Finish everything at the table: basil, cilantro, sliced red onion, and arugula right before it hits a plate.

## Substitutions

- For the jackfruit, use shredded vegan chicken, leftover pulled pork, or button mushrooms torn into strands. Mushrooms only need about 4 minutes in the skillet.
- BBQ sauce is the easiest swap point. Bottled, homemade, or even a quick glaze of ketchup, maple syrup, and a splash of vinegar all work.
- No pizza stone? Bake on the heaviest sheet pan you own, preheated for the same 20 minutes.
- Any fruit caramelizes the same way pineapple does, so peaches, pears, or thin apple slices are all fair game.
- Vegan mozzarella is optional. Leave it off and you get a lighter, less rich pie that still holds together.
- For more heat, add a tablespoon of sliced pickled jalapeños with the pineapple.
