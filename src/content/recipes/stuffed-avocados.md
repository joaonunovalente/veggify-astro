## Why you'll love this recipe

Avocados are one of the best things to eat cold on a hot day, and once you halve them and fill them with something, they stop being a snack and start being a proper side dish. This is the version I make when lunch needs to be on the table in fifteen minutes and the kitchen is already too warm to stand over a stove.

The filling is quinoa, corn, and cherry tomatoes, which keeps it light and gives it enough substance to sit next to grilled fish or a pile of beans. The dressing is where the flavor sits: lots of lime, smoked paprika, cumin, and a little olive oil, with a pinch of cayenne if you want the heat.

Because everything is served at room temperature, these are as good an hour later as they are straight away, which makes them the perfect make-ahead dish.

## How to make this recipe

Toast the cumin first, in a dry skillet, for about half a minute. Ground spices go stale and dusty in the jar, and thirty seconds in a hot pan brings them right back. Watch the pan, not the clock, and pull them the moment they smell sweet rather than burnt.

Add the lime juice and oil to the same pan, then the quinoa and corn. Taste for salt here, before the avocados go anywhere near it, since you cannot season the filling properly once it is stuffed into a cavity.

Score the avocado flesh in a crosshatch without cutting through the skin. It looks decorative, but it gives the avocado a much larger surface area for the lime and oil to soak into, and it makes the filling easier to scoop out with a fork at the table.

Use the leftover flesh you scoop out. Toss it into the quinoa with the diced tomatoes, because there is no reason to waste the best part of an avocado.

## Substitutions

- No quinoa? Cooked brown rice, couscous, or even leftover rice all work.
- Swap the corn for white beans or chickpeas for more protein and a better lunch.
- Cilantro instead of nothing, plus a tablespoon of chopped scallion, makes the filling brighter.
- For a richer version, add 2 Tbsp of crumbled feta or vegan feta before stuffing.
- If your avocados are not ripe enough to eat today, plate them with the filling on the side and let everyone assemble their own.
- Try this with watermelon or cantaloupe in place of the tomatoes in summer.
