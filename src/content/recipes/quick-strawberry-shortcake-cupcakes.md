## Why you'll love this recipe

Strawberry shortcake should taste like early June: sweet, a little tart, and nothing fussy. This is my small-batch version of it, the one I make when I want something from-scratch without committing a whole afternoon to a cake.

The batter is a straightforward cream-method cupcake with a good amount of fine sugar, which keeps the crumb tender and slightly crumbly, the way shortcake should be. Almond extract is optional but it does lean the whole thing toward a shortcake rather than a plain sweet cupcake. The macerated strawberries go in between the layers, not on top, so every bite gets some.

There is no frosting shortcut here either — buttercream takes about five minutes with a stand mixer and completely changes the result. Plus, at four cupcakes, this is a recipe you can make on a whim.

## How to make this recipe

Everything comes down to how you mix and how little you fuss with the batter. Beat the butter and sugar for a full two minutes until it looks aerated and pale. That air is what gives you a light crumb rather than a dense little brick.

Fold the dry ingredients in with a spatula, in three additions if you can, and stop the moment there are no visible streaks of flour. Then stop mixing. The lumps are fine; overworked gluten is what turns cupcakes chewy.

Cool them completely before cutting. Warm cake and wet filling combine into mush at the seam, and the buttercream will slide straight off a warm cupcake.

The strawberries macerate while the oven does its work, which is the whole reason the timing on this recipe works out to under an hour start to finish.

## Substitutions

- Swap almond extract for a little more vanilla, or leave it out entirely for a plainer vanilla cupcake.
- Use any firm berry. Raspberries are excellent, and blueberries hold their shape better than strawberries do.
- For a gluten-free version, replace the flour cup-for-cup with a 1:1 gluten-free blend and do not overmix.
- Leftover cupcakes keep for 2 days at room temperature, or a week in the freezer. Buttercream frosts best straight from the fridge.
- Make it a shortcake sandwich by skipping the frosting and layering two cupcakes with strawberries and a spoonful of whipped vegan cream between them.
